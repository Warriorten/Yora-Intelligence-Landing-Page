About Yora

Making supplement intelligence more accessible, reliable and actionable.

The global supplement industry offers an enormous selection of products, yet understanding true formulation quality remains difficult. Product information is spread across manufacturers, labels, regulatory databases and other sources.

Yora was created to address this information gap.

Our platform resolves products, normalizes ingredients and dosages, assesses formulation quality independently from verification evidence, and explains the results in a way teams can act on.

At the centre is our two-score architecture: Product Score for formulation quality and Verification Score for supporting evidence — so certifications alone are never mistaken for superior formulation.

Evidence & trust

Authoritative inputs. Proprietary intelligence.

Yora draws on recognized regulatory and scientific sources, then applies proprietary analysis to produce source-traceable, confidence-aware outputs for enterprise workflows.

  • Health Canada NHP monographs

    Authoritative natural health product monograph references.

  • LNHPD / NPN

    Licensed Natural Health Products Database and product numbers.

  • NIH Office of Dietary Supplements

    Scientific and educational dietary supplement resources.

  • NIH / IOM Dietary Reference Intakes

    Reference intake frameworks used in nutrition science.

  • NIH Dietary Supplement Label Database

    Label-level dietary supplement information for product intelligence.

Source-traceable outputs

Results are designed to show the evidence and inputs that inform each assessment — not black-box conclusions.

Confidence-aware analysis

Where evidence is limited or unavailable, Yora communicates that limitation clearly rather than implying failure.

Important: Yora’s Product Score and Verification Score are proprietary analytical outputs. They are not Health Canada ratings, approvals or certifications.
